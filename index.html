Networked Systems Group, ETH Zurich

A measurement study on whether Internet flows actually compete for bandwidth. I am developing a congestion control algorithm that detects path contention and extending existing active measurement techniques.

Dec 2025 – present

Distributed storage in LEO satellite networks

Guided research project, Chair of Connected Mobility, TU Munich

Taking a published in-orbit distributed storage system (LEO-NET ’25) from an idealized simulator to packet-level ns-3 simulations with realistic orbital dynamics.

I identified the constraints that break the original design and developed a ring-based routing algorithm, with a conflict-free protocol for relocating the routing structure as satellites move.
